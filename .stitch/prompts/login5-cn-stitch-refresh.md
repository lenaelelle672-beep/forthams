# LOGIN5 中文产品图登录页复刻

[Context & Style]
Desktop web login screen for UNIVIEW 固定资产平台. This is the LOGIN5 design-draft route, not a dashboard page.
Use a high-end B2B intelligent manufacturing visual style: deep navy smart factory product illustration, CNC machines, robotic arms, conveyors, AGV carts, IoT signal nodes, holographic Chinese telemetry panels, crisp blue/cyan lighting, and a clean white login panel.
Brand text must be UNIVIEW. Product text must be 固定资产平台 / 固定资产智能运维中枢. All visible copy in the factory/product image should be Chinese. Do not use English phrases like Login Page, Smart factory administration, art factory, or lorem ipsum.

[Layout]
Full viewport split layout with a clear vertical division:
- Left 66%: one immersive product image block filling the whole left side, no card frame, no grid background. The product image must feel like a polished IMAGE2 product visual, not a generic stock photo.
- Right 34%: white login panel from top to bottom, compact and professional, aligned to the right area, with generous but not excessive padding.
- Keep the dividing line clear and straight. Avoid floating cards over the image except subtle Chinese telemetry panels inside the product image.

[Components]
Left product image text:
- Large title: 固定资产智能运维中枢
- Subtitle: 智能制造资产全生命周期管理
- Supporting line: 连接 MES、设备状态、维保工单与安全态势
- Holographic panels: 设备运行监控、资产健康分析、安全态势感知、维保工单管理、巡检执行看板

Right login panel:
- Small shield logo mark
- Heading: 登录系统
- Subheading: 使用组织账号进入固定资产平台
- Username field placeholder: 请输入账号
- Password field placeholder: 请输入密码
- Checkbox: 记住用户名
- Link: 忘记密码?
- Primary button: 登录并进入工作台
- Secondary SSO button: UNIVIEW 统一身份认证
- Demo account section with four compact tiles: 系统管理员、资产管理员、部门负责人、运维人员
- Footer: 查看工作台预览 and 2026 UNIVIEW

[Constraints]
Do not show dashboard KPI cards such as 资产总数、在线运行、待保数、异常预警 on the login page.
Do not use visible drop shadows on primary UI cards; use thin borders and subtle tonal surfaces.
No legacy SEELINK or forthAMS branding.
No English in the product image except UNIVIEW, MES, and numerals.
